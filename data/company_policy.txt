Acme Analytics Remote Work Policy

Acme Analytics allows full-time employees to work remotely up to three days per week after their first 30 days of employment. Managers may approve additional remote days for project needs, health accommodations, or travel disruption.

Employees must be reachable between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their local time zone. Meetings should include written agendas and action items. Confidential client data must only be accessed through the company VPN and approved devices.

The policy was last reviewed in March 2026 by the People Operations team.
