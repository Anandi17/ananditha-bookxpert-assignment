Release Notes: Version 2.4

Version 2.4 added dashboard filters, CSV export for reports, and faster workspace search. Search latency improved by approximately 35 percent on workspaces with more than 50,000 notes.

The release also fixed a bug where archived notebooks appeared in the mobile quick switcher. A known limitation remains: comments are not included in CSV exports.

Version 2.4 was released on May 12, 2026.
