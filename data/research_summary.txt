Research Summary: Customer Support Automation

A pilot project tested an AI assistant for customer support triage. The assistant categorized incoming tickets, suggested knowledge base articles, and routed complex issues to human agents.

During the six-week pilot, first response time decreased from 8 hours to 2 hours. Human agents reported that the assistant was most helpful for password reset, billing status, and product navigation requests.

The research team recommended expanding the pilot only after improving escalation detection for angry or high-risk customers.
